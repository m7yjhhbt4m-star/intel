# Boat Harbour / A’se’k — Source Register and Evidence Quality

**Version:** 2.0 — 21 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Trace the evidence used in the comprehensive review and identify what each source can and cannot establish.

## Evidence labels

- **Primary regulatory source:** Government decision, assessment, monitoring or enforcement record.
- **Primary scientific source:** Peer-reviewed study reporting methods and results.
- **Project-proponent source:** Build Nova Scotia or consultant document; important but not independent verification.
- **Secondary reporting:** Media or synthesis; useful for leads/status, not a substitute for primary records.
- **Model output:** Prediction or scenario, not a measured environmental result.

## Source hierarchy and interpretation rules

1. Prefer original peer-reviewed papers and government records over secondary summaries.
2. Distinguish measured sample results from model predictions, project estimates and risk-assessment assumptions.
3. Do not merge sediment inventory estimates with waste-cell capacity or modelled suspended-solid volume.
4. Do not extrapolate a study beyond its sampled area, time period, media or analytes.
5. Preserve qualifiers, detection limits, dry/wet basis and guideline version.
6. When a source table is internally inconsistent or a number is figure-derived, flag it rather than silently repairing it.
7. A project-proponent report is valuable for project status but is not an independent environmental finding.
