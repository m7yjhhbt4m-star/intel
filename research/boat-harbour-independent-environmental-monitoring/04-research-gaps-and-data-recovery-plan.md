## 2. Questions requiring explicit verification

### Sediment
- What is the best current estimate of total sediment/sludge volume in the lagoon, settling basins, wetlands and estuary?
- Which volume estimates refer to lagoon sediment, total unconsolidated sediment, existing cell waste, proposed remediation waste or modelled suspended solids? They are not interchangeable.
- What is the actual vertical profile below 15 cm at historical hotspots and in the western lagoon?
- Are there sediment layers with different contaminant signatures or geogenic backgrounds?
- Which locations have been sampled using cores versus grabs, and which samples were composites?

### Wetlands and watercourses
- Which of the 25 mapped wetlands have actual chemistry data?
- What were the exact sample depths and hydrological conditions?
- Which wetlands were directly exposed to early raw effluent, and which were only potentially influenced by later runoff or hydrological connectivity?
- What is the current condition of the historic 1967–1972 raw-effluent wetland route?
- What is the current condition of the 2014 spill receiving area?

### Estuary and Northumberland Strait
- What is the full station footprint of the 2020 baseline, and how does it compare to the 2025 IAAC marine study boundaries?
- Which 2003 marine sediment samples are still available, and where were they taken?
- What are the current benthic habitat and eelgrass distributions?
- Are there reliable tidal/current models and bathymetry datasets for the estuary mouth and embayment?
- What seasonal or storm-driven transport pathways have been measured rather than modelled?

### Biota and human exposure
- What are the exact species, tissue types, age classes, locations and sample sizes in all historical biota studies?
- Is total arsenic speciated in any of the lobster datasets?
- Which mercury results are total Hg versus methylmercury?
- What food-web or sediment-porewater data exist to explain MeHg?
- Which community food-use data or country-food monitoring results can be shared under PLFN direction and data governance?

### Remediation and containment cell
- What is the status of the judicial review and alternative site process after November 2026?
- Have any revised follow-up programmes been prepared or approved?
- Has an independent environmental monitor been retained?
- Have any project site-preparation or monitoring activities occurred since the 2025 annual report?
- What is the latest containment-cell design, geotechnical verification, leachate treatment design and water monitoring plan?

## 4. Quality-control checklist for each recovered dataset

- Source organization, author, report title, date and version.
- Original file and immutable copy/hash.
- Sampling date/time and station coordinates.
- Coordinate reference system and accuracy.
- Medium, sample type, depth interval and collection method.
- Analyte, fraction/speciation, result, unit and dry/wet-weight basis.
- Detection limit, reporting limit, qualifier and uncertainty.
- Lab identity, accreditation scope and method number.
- Field/lab blanks, duplicates, spikes and reference materials.
- Guideline name, version, medium and jurisdiction.
- Whether the result is a measurement, estimate, model output or secondary-data transcription.
- Whether the sample is representative of the stratum or a targeted hotspot.
- Whether the result can be compared with other years/methods.

## 5. Stop rules

- Do not make a contamination-spread claim from a modelled plume or a single anomalous sample.
- Do not call the Strait clean based on a limited near-field baseline.
- Do not combine incompatible sediment-volume estimates.
- Do not compare concentrations without checking units, dry/wet basis, fraction and detection limit.
- Do not use a figure-derived approximate value as a precise laboratory result.
- Do not treat total arsenic as inorganic arsenic.
- Do not treat DGT-labile concentration as total sediment concentration.
- Do not treat no reported project monitoring as evidence of compliance.
- Do not finalize sampling stations before PLFN engagement, access and GIS reconstruction.
