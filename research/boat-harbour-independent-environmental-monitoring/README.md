## Evidence standards

- Label facts as measured results, source-reported findings, model predictions, estimates, interpretations or proposals.
- Never claim that a contaminant has spread beyond sampled areas without direct evidence.
- Never claim the wider Northumberland Strait is clean based on a small near-field sample set.
- Remote sensing is for optical proxies, mapping and reconnaissance; chemical identification requires laboratory analysis.
- DGT is a labile/bioavailable fraction measure, not a total contaminant inventory.
- Sonar maps bathymetry and bottom structure, not chemical composition.
- Preserve original raw data, lab certificates, detection limits, qualifiers, metadata and chain of custody.
- Engage Pictou Landing First Nation and follow its direction on research governance, access, sensitive data and publication before fieldwork.

## Status

<badge>Desk research package — version 3.3</badge>

This package is a substantial but incomplete source-based desk review and proposed technical framework. The registry contains five EIS volumes, Appendices A–EE, 82 Round 1 information requirements, Round 2 records and public comments; not all have been systematically reviewed. The new registry audit distinguishes located records from reviewed content and unrecovered raw data. The Indigenous and rights-holder evidence stream now includes full-text review of five primary submissions; underlying technical reports, referendum records, land instruments, full Appendix S/T studies and the official 2025 Annual Report remain outstanding. The full Hive Engineering IR82 report and the July 2022 GHD/NSLands technical/economic feasibility assessment have now been reviewed, along with the February 2024 Build Nova Scotia response. The comparative review identifies a material GHG scenario-attribution mismatch, transport-distance ambiguity, cost-scope/arithmetic inconsistencies, and unresolved groundwater, parcel and field-investigation questions. The groundwater review identifies a material draft-to-final condition change: draft condition 5.7 expressly required wellfield groundwater quality/quantity and static water-level monitoring, while the final Decision Statement does not contain that standalone groundwater condition; provincial approvals and approved project plans remain to be checked. Underlying 2010/2016 groundwater reports and the 2018 GHG assessment are now explicit source leads, not yet reviewed. A draft litigation-supporting evidence standard has been added but is not yet implemented or approved by counsel. The GitHub repository's visibility has not been independently verified; until the owner confirms it, treat it as public and not as an evidence vault. Do not describe this package as a completed environmental investigation until the outstanding records, data, current project/legal status and any authorized field programme are addressed.
