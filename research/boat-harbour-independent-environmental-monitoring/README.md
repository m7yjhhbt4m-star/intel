## Evidence standards

- Label facts as measured results, source-reported findings, model predictions, estimates, interpretations or proposals.
- Never claim that a contaminant has spread beyond sampled areas without direct evidence.
- Never claim the wider Northumberland Strait is clean based on a small near-field sample set.
- Remote sensing is for optical proxies, mapping and reconnaissance; chemical identification requires laboratory analysis.
- DGT is a labile/bioavailable fraction measure, not a total contaminant inventory.
- Sonar maps bathymetry and bottom structure, not chemical composition.
- Preserve original raw data, lab certificates, detection limits, qualifiers, metadata and chain of custody.
- Engage Pictou Landing First Nation and follow its direction on research governance, access, sensitive data and publication before fieldwork.

## Status

<badge>Desk research package — version 2.7</badge>

This package is a substantial but incomplete source-based desk review and proposed technical framework. The registry contains five EIS volumes, Appendices A–EE, 82 Round 1 information requirements, Round 2 records and public comments; not all have been systematically reviewed. The new registry audit distinguishes located records from reviewed content and unrecovered raw data. The Indigenous and rights-holder evidence stream is an initial build: several original PLFN attachments and the full Appendix S/T materials remain to be retrieved and reviewed. Do not describe this package as a completed environmental investigation until the outstanding records, data, current project/legal status and any authorized field programme are addressed.

## Latest review pass — 25 November 2026

Version 2.7 adds direct reviews of the 2018 GHD Laboratory Treatability Study, the 2018 Supplemental Phase II ESA, the 2018 Remedial Option Decision Document, EIS Volumes I–III, the 2016 draft BHEAC terms, the 2020 draft EMP/PEPP package, and the 2021 SCG/WSP Sludge Thickness Determination presentation. The source register and audit record distinguish the full original reports reviewed from secondary summaries and access blockers.

Important new findings include localized petroleum-hydrocarbon sediment exceedances in five samples at the Northumberland Strait estuary mouth (with further delineation still recommended); laboratory reporting limits above criteria for several treated-water analytes; differences between the 2018 and 2020 remediation strategies; uncertainty in waste-volume definitions and Geotube scale-up; and the need to distinguish LIF/EC material mapping from compound-specific chemical analysis.

**Still not directly reviewed:** EIS Appendix A / HHERA (137871E), Appendix C / public meeting minutes (137873E), the original Phase I and Phase II ESAs, the 580-page 2019 GHD Pilot Scale Testing Construction Report, and the full Sludge Thickness Determination report/raw LIF/EC dataset. The Supplemental Phase II ESA's accessible Appendix D lab-certificate section is a placeholder, not the certificates. See documents 05, 07, 08 and 09 for exact statuses and follow-up.
